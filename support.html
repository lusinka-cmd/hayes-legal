HAYes Support

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Thank you for playing HAYes, an Armenian word game developed by Lusine Karapetyan.

Contact Support

For help, bug reports, privacy questions, or other support requests, email support.hayes@gmail.com.

For technical problems, please include when possible your device model, iOS or Android version, HAYes app version/build, a short description, steps that led to the problem, and a screenshot if it can be shared without sensitive information.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

Gameplay Help

HAYes is an Armenian word game in which you identify groups of four words connected by a common idea. If a gameplay problem continues after restarting the app, contact support and include the game number when available.

Challenge and Sharing Help

HAYes may allow result sharing or challenges. Shared-link behavior can depend on device, operating system, installed app version, and platform capabilities. If a challenge does not open correctly, send support the challenge link with device and app-version information.

Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or data-related requests, email support.hayes@gmail.com. Because HAYes does not currently require accounts, some automatically generated analytics or diagnostic information may not be reasonably identifiable as belonging to a particular person.
