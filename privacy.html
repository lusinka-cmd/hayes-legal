Privacy Policy for HAYes

Last updated: September 2, 2026

HAYes (“HAYes”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) is an Armenian word game developed by Lusine Karapetyan. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to understand app usage, maintain app stability, provide app functionality, and improve the HAYes experience.

1. Information We Collect

HAYes does not require users to create an account and does not directly request or collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, financial information, or precise location as part of normal gameplay.

The app may automatically collect limited technical, diagnostic, and usage information through services described in this policy, including device or installation identifiers used for analytics, diagnostics, and app functionality; product interaction and app usage information; and crash reports and diagnostic information.

HAYes does not use this information to determine your real-world identity.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to understand app usage, measure interactions with features, improve gameplay and user experience, diagnose technical problems, maintain reliability, security and performance, and develop HAYes features. We do not sell personal information.

3. Analytics

HAYes uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics, to understand how users interact with the app. Analytics information may include app interactions, gameplay or feature events, technical device information, and device or installation identifiers. We use this information for analytics and product improvement.

4. Crash Reporting

HAYes uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify, diagnose, and resolve crashes and technical problems. Crashlytics may process crash reports, diagnostic information, technical device information, and installation identifiers.

5. Tracking and Advertising

HAYes does not use the information described in this policy to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes. HAYes does not currently use third-party advertising networks to provide targeted advertising based on activity across other companies’ apps or websites.

6. Data Linked to Your Identity

HAYes does not currently require an account or login for normal gameplay. We do not intentionally associate analytics, diagnostic, or gameplay information with your name, email address, or another directly identifying HAYes account. Technical services may use device or installation identifiers to provide analytics, diagnostics, security, or related functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

HAYes uses services provided by Google Firebase, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may process technical, analytics, diagnostic, device, and installation information in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy practices.

8. Children’s Privacy

HAYes is a general-audience Armenian word game and is not designed to require children to provide personal information. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it, including deletion where required.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information processed in connection with HAYes. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics, technical, and diagnostic information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here and subject to the retention settings and practices of services used by HAYes. Different Firebase services may apply different retention periods.

Because HAYes does not currently provide user accounts, we may not always be able to associate automatically generated information with a specific individual. You may nevertheless contact us to ask questions about retention or request deletion of information that can reasonably be identified and deleted.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide rights concerning your personal data, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other appropriate action. You may also contact us to withdraw previously provided consent where applicable.

12. International Processing

Third-party technology services used by HAYes may process information on servers located outside your country or region, subject to applicable privacy practices, safeguards, and law.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HAYes features, technologies, third-party services, privacy practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy and updated date will be published on this page.
