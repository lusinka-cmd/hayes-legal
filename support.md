# HAYes Support

**Last updated: September 2, 2026**

Thank you for playing HAYes, an Armenian word game developed by Lusine Karapetyan.

## Contact Support

For help with HAYes, bug reports, privacy questions, or other support requests, contact:

**Email:** support.hayes@gmail.com

When reporting a technical problem, please include, when possible:

- Your device model;
- iOS or Android version;
- HAYes app version/build;
- A short description of what happened;
- The steps that led to the problem;
- A screenshot, if it can be shared without revealing sensitive information.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information in a support request.

## Gameplay Help

HAYes is an Armenian word game in which you identify groups of four words connected by a common idea. The app may include daily games, hints, difficulty levels, streaks, statistics, result sharing, and challenges.

If you encounter a gameplay problem, restarting the app may resolve temporary issues. If the problem continues, contact support and include the game number when available.

## Challenge and Sharing Help

HAYes may allow you to share results or send a challenge to another person. Availability and behavior of shared links can depend on the recipient’s device, operating system, installed app version, and platform capabilities.

If a challenge does not open correctly, please send support the challenge link together with the device and app-version information, but do not include unrelated private information.
